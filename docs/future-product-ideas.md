# Future product ideas

## Avatar Match

A lightweight AI-assisted discovery layer that helps users find VRM avatars matching their personality and preferences. The model should only rank and explain candidates grounded in catalog evidence.

Inputs can include style, species, mood, colors, realism vs stylization, presentation, budget, chain preference, licensing needs, and whether the avatar is currently obtainable.

Candidate evidence should include normalized NFT traits, collection descriptions, avatar names, visual-style tags, rarity/frequency signals, validated VRM status, usage/license restrictions, holder gating, marketplace availability, and purchase/collection URLs.

The product can support both collection matching and individual-avatar matching. Conversational feedback such as “less human,” “cheaper,” “stranger,” or “more like this” can rerank results.

## Trait Rarity

A minimal MMO-inspired rarity treatment for avatar properties, modeled after ATLREO's explainable tier/color pattern rather than a hidden composite score.

Start from objective observed trait frequency within collections whose inventory coverage is sufficient:

`trait frequency = avatars with trait / avatars with trait data`

Suggested labels: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary. Use restrained color accents and percentages beside traits. Keep trait rarity distinct from market value and do not invent an overall NFT rarity score unless the collection has a defensible methodology.

Potential extensions include avatar rarity profiles, rarity filters, collection distribution views, and rarity as one preference signal for Avatar Match.
