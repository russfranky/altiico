# Brand guardrails
Use `docs/reference/visuals/source-reference.png` as the primary visual authority.

Keep near-black surfaces, off-white type, cyan-teal signal color, thin rules, monospace labels, archive motifs, and sparse alignment.

Do not add multi-color gradients, glassmorphism, rounded consumer-SaaS cards, unrelated illustration styles, or bright gaming palettes.

Use `docs/brand/BENTO_LAYOUT.md` for the approved bento page-composition direction. Bento panels are large functional page zones, not permission to turn every nested component into a card.
