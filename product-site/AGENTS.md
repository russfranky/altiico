# Product-site rules

- Preserve the established Altiico palette and technical visual language.
- Use near-black surfaces, off-white text, and cyan-teal as the only strong accent.
- Use bento composition as layout, not as a new brand.
- Keep quiet navigation on the background layer when the viewport permits it.
- Keep product IDs and route slugs separate from evidence identifiers.
- Treat root catalog output as read-only evidence through adapters.
- Do not move secrets, blockchain indexing, binary validation, or publishing authority into the browser.
- Do not change the public deployment from this branch without review.
